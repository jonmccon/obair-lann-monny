## Western Washington University's graphic design program lived up in Bellingham — a beautiful campus town, yes, but not exactly a place where the Seattle creative community was going to wander in on a Thursday night to see senior work.

So we brought the show to them.

A Few from Western was a self-organized graduating exhibition, planned by a small group of students who wanted their work seen somewhere it could actually be seen. We secured the Tether Gallery in Pioneer Square — a proper creative space in the heart of Seattle's design district — and filled it with single projects from 16 seniors. One piece each, no dilution: the kind of editing that forces a student to lead with their best idea.

The event itself was a hit. Packed house, genuine conversations, a guest book that got out of hand in the best way. It wasn't a school-sanctioned showcase or a polished department send-off. It was a bunch of designers who rented a van and made something happen.

The branding leaned into that energy: big, direct, typographically loud. "A few things we learned while spending too much time in the art department." The website was built to match — informal, funny, honest.

Design and production in collaboration with Riley Hoonan, Glenn Newcomer, photography from Erik Simkins. Special thanks to Daniel Petrzelka & Bebet Caguin of Tether, and the WWU Class of 2010.
